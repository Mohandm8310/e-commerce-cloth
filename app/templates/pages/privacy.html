Privacy & Data Governance Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

StyleSphere collects minimal necessary personal information to process fashion orders and provide OTP-based customer authentication. This includes your email address, phone number, shipping coordinates, and transaction references.

2. Cryptographic Security & OTPs

One-time passwords (OTPs) are hashed using salted SHA-256 algorithms and expire strictly within 10 minutes. We never store raw payment credentials, credit card details, or CVV codes; all payments are processed through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

3. Customer Session Analytics

Our administrator analytics monitor active sessions for capacity planning and security auditing. We do not sell, rent, or trade your shopping history or personal identifiers to third-party ad networks.

4. Data Subject Rights

You may request deletion or export of your account profile and addresses at any time by contacting privacy@stylesphere.com.
